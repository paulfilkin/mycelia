# Mycelia help

How to use Mycelia, a program for recording your family tree and researching it.  In the program,
press **F1** to open the page for the screen you're on, or use Main menu > Help.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): installing, the sample tree, starting your own tree,
  importing a GEDCOM file, saving and undo.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md)
- [Questions and troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)

## Recording your tree

- [The People workspace](people.md): the index, the tree, the person panel and the Person view.
- [Facts, dates and sources](facts-and-sources.md): adding facts, writing dates, citing sources,
  notes.
- [Places](places.md)
- [Sources](sources.md)
- [Media](media.md): pictures and documents.
- [Charts](charts.md): pedigree, descendant, fan and family group charts to save or print.
- [Tools](tools.md): relationship and date calculators, Soundex, duplicates and merging, data
  checks, find and replace.
- [GEDCOM, merging and backups](gedcom-and-backups.md): moving trees in and out, merging trees,
  backing up with media.
- [Options and updates](options-and-updates.md)

## Researching with the Assistant

- [The Assistant](assistant.md): choosing an AI model, asking, approving changes, what the model
  sees, researching records.
- [MCP servers](mcp-servers.md): connecting archives and other services, and the research
  settings.
- [Other programs](other-programs.md): letting Claude Desktop, Claude Code and other programs use
  Mycelia's tools.
